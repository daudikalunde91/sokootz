# SokoTZ — Project Brief

## 1. Project Overview
SokoTZ is a Tanzania-first business directory and marketplace that helps customers discover businesses, products and services. The platform should be designed to support expansion into other countries in the future.

## 2. Problem Statement
Customers may struggle to discover local businesses and compare available products or services. Small businesses may lack an affordable and accessible online presence.

## 3. Vision
Build a trusted, accessible and scalable platform connecting customers with businesses, starting in Tanzania and expanding internationally when the product and business are ready.

## 4. Target Users
- Visitors searching for businesses, products and services.
- Customers who want to contact businesses.
- Business owners managing profiles and listings.
- Administrators and moderators maintaining platform quality.

## 5. Core Product
- Business profiles with contact information, location and descriptions.
- Product and service listings with images where supported.
- Search and filters by name, category and location.
- Direct contact through phone and WhatsApp.
- Administrative review, reporting and moderation.
- Future subscription plans and sponsored listings.

### 5.1 SokoTZ Assistant (Planned supporting feature)
SokoTZ Assistant is a planned AI-powered chat assistant inside the marketplace. The marketplace remains the main product; the assistant supports it and does not replace search, listings or direct contact. The detailed reference is [SOKOTZ_ASSISTANT.md](SOKOTZ_ASSISTANT.md).

- Purpose: make SokoTZ easier to use by helping people find real businesses, products and services, answering platform questions, and helping business owners write listing text.
- Target users: visitors and customers (discovery and help), business owners (help and drafting). Administrator features are not planned for the MVP.
- Value to customers: describe what they need in everyday Kiswahili or English and get links to real listings.
- Value to sellers: faster, clearer product descriptions and promotional text, drafted from details the seller supplies.
- Value to the platform: less repetitive support effort and better listing quality. These are expected benefits to be validated, not established facts.
- MVP scope: chat interface, platform help, search grounded in real marketplace data, links to real listings, seller drafting assistance, clear error handling and privacy protections.
- Limitations: the assistant never invents businesses, prices, availability, ratings, reviews, locations or statistics and says when something cannot be verified. It cannot take consequential actions such as publishing, purchasing or paying. It depends on an AI provider that has not been selected and adds usage cost that has not been estimated.
- Status: Planned. Advanced autonomous actions, payments and personalization are future work unless a recorded decision says otherwise.

## 6. Business Model
Potential revenue sources include paid business plans and sponsored placements. Prices, demand and willingness to pay must be validated before being treated as established facts.

## 7. Geographic Strategy
Launch in Tanzania. Design the system for future country, currency, language and location support without prematurely building every international feature.

## 8. MVP Principles
Prioritize usable core features, reliable account management, real database persistence, security and mobile-friendly design. Avoid unnecessary complexity.

## 9. Success Measures
Track registered businesses, active listings, successful searches, contact clicks, returning users, listing quality and validated paying customers.

**PROPOSED — NEEDS APPROVAL** additional measures for SokoTZ Assistant, to be recorded only after it exists: assistant conversations started, share of conversations that lead to a listing link click or contact click, share of searches with no results, assistant error rate, seller drafts requested, user feedback where collected, and assistant usage cost per active user.

## 10. Project Rules
Requirements, architecture, security and implementation status must be documented. Distinguish confirmed decisions, assumptions, planned features and implemented features.

## 11. Scope Control
New features must be evaluated against user value, cost, dependencies and MVP priorities before implementation.

SokoTZ Assistant is evaluated under the same rule: it follows the staged plan in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) and does not delay or replace the core marketplace stages it depends on.
